# KaraOkay

Karaokay is an online karaoke website where users can enjoy singing their favorite songs anytime, anywhere. It provides a fun and interactive way to experience karaoke directly through your browser, whether you're singing solo or having fun with friends.


# Features
Song Library – Browse and choose from a collection of karaoke songs.
Search Songs – Easily find your favorite songs.
Online Karaoke – Sing along to your favorite music directly from your browser.
Lyrics Display – Follow the lyrics while singing.
Music Playback – Play, pause, and control karaoke songs.
Responsive Design – Enjoy karaoke on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
